Cancer Screening Programmes
ATS Brianza Area
BOWEL WHO IS IT FOR?: Men and women aged from 50 to 74 living in Lecco, Monza or
province.
CANCER
HOW DOES IT WORK?: You will receive a letter every 2 years if your last test was
SCREENING normal. Take the letter to a local pharmacy to collect your free home test kit.

WHO IS IT FOR?: Women aged from 45 to 74 living in Lecco, Monza or BREAST
province. CANCER
HOW DOES IT WORK?: Ages from 45 to 49: invited every year. Ages from 50 SCREENING
to 74: invited every 2 years. (MAMMOGRAM)
You will receive a letter if your last test was normal.

WHO IS IT FOR?: Men and women born between 1969 and 1989 (unless already
(HCV) treated or tested).
SCREENING HOW DOES IT WORK?: Go to an authorised hospital blood test centre or
accredited laboratory with your invitation letter. Only one blood sample is needed.

WHO IS IT FOR?:
For women:
- Ages from 25 to 29: Pap smear every 3 years. CERVICAL
- Ages from 30 to 64: HPV test every 5 years. CANCER
You will receive an invitation letter if your last result was normal. SCREENING
The test is quick and usually painless.

WHO IS IT FOR?: Men registered with a General Practitioner in Lombardy, aged
PROSTATE between 50 and 64 years.
CANCER From the age of 50 to the age of 70.
SCREENING HOWDOES IT WORK?: Complete the questionnaire in your Electronic Health
(PSA TEST) Record (FSE) To check if you can have the PSA blood test.
